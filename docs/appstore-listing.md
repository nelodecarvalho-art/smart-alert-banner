## FULL DESCRIPTION

```
Smart Alert Banner shows the right message to the right customer — automatically.
Every visitor sees a banner tailored to their US state, driving urgency with
personalized offers. Everyone else browses normally, keeping your store clean
and your conversions focused where it matters.

KEY FEATURES

✅ Target any of the 50 US states + Washington D.C.
Pick exactly which state sees your banner. Visitors from every other state
browse normally — no clutter, no irrelevant messages.

⏰ Live countdown timer
Add a deadline and let urgency do the work. The timer ticks down in real time
on the banner and hides it automatically when the offer expires.

🎨 Fully customizable design
Set your banner text, background color, and text color to match your brand.
Toggle the state label ("📍 CA only") on or off with a single click.

👁️ Real-time live preview
See exactly how your banner looks before it goes live. Use the built-in preview
link to test on your real storefront from any location in the world.

🛒 Zero code, zero theme edits
Smart Alert Banner installs as a Shopify App Embed Block. Enable it in your
Theme Customizer and you're live — no developer, no liquid edits, no hassle.

📍 Automatic geo-detection
Your visitor's state is detected automatically via IP on every page load.
No cookies, no opt-ins, no friction — it just works.

🔒 Non-intrusive by default
Visitors can dismiss the banner with one click. Once closed, it stays hidden
for the rest of their session — no annoying repeat pop-ups.

POPULAR USE CASES

• Free shipping for a specific state:
  "🎉 Free 2-day shipping for California customers — today only!"

• Flash sale targeting a region:
  "⚡ Texas Flash Sale — 20% off everything, ends midnight!"

• Sales tax holiday alerts (back-to-school, clothing exemptions):
  "🛍️ No sales tax this weekend — Florida shoppers save more!"

• Low stock or local inventory announcements:
  "📦 Limited stock available for New York — order before it's gone!"

• Seasonal and event-driven campaigns:
  "❄️ Winter Storm Special — free delivery across the Northeast!"

• Age-restriction or compliance notices for regulated states

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install Smart Alert Banner from the Shopify App Store.
2. Open the app, write your message, choose a target state, and pick your colors.
3. Go to Online Store → Themes → Customize → App Embeds and toggle on Smart Alert Banner.
4. Hit Save — your banner is live in seconds.

That's the entire setup. No code. No theme edits. No developer required.
```
